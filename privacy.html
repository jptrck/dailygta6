DailyGTA6 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

DailyGTA6 Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the DailyGTA6 channel. It compiles publicly available GTA 6 Twitch clips into videos and uploads them to the DailyGTA6 accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is used only by its operator.

What data the app uses

  • YouTube / Google: an OAuth token for the DailyGTA6 Google account, used only to upload videos and thumbnails to that channel, and the account's email address to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • TikTok: an OAuth token and basic profile info (open ID, display name) for the DailyGTA6 TikTok account, used only to upload videos to that account.
  • Twitch: public clip information (clip titles, view counts, streamer names) from the Twitch API, used to choose and credit clips.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from viewers or other users.

Removal requests

Streamers who want their clips excluded or removed can contact dailygta6daily@gmail.com, and the clips will be removed and blocked from future videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page or TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.
